# work

A piece of work and the only place its state changes. Pure.

Not built. `types.ts` is the contract and compiles; `service.ts` says so where it is called. What it must do: spec W1 to W13 in
[docs/spec.md](../../../docs/spec.md). What shows it: the evals under `work` in [docs/book.md](../../../docs/book.md).

## Settled by the evals

Where the spec and `types.ts` leave a reading open, the evals in `evals/rules/work*.eval.ts` take this one.

- `take` puts work on the kind's first step (`step` is that step's id) and starts it: an agent step gets a `start` (role `does`, a brief that names the title), a person step an `ask` with a date; with `waits` the step is `triage` and nothing starts. `next` is `itself`, dated `at` plus the kind's `look` (`45m`, `4h`, `3d`), `follows` is `[from]`, and a `wake` to-do is at `next.by`.
- `stepOf` is the kind's step with the work's `step` id; `triage`, `done` and unknown ids have none. `columns` is `triage` (Triage), each step id once in the order met over the kinds, then `done` (Done), each with a name and a tip.
- A to-do's `key` is made from the event's key, so the same event gives the same keys, and two events never share one. Whenever a move sets a new `next` on open work, a `wake` to-do is at its date. The id of an ask made by a to-do is that to-do's `key`, and `waits.ask` is that id.
- Seen again: the work has no record of keys, so the same event applied to the work it made changes nothing and gives no to-do whose key was not given before (`Same`, a `Refused`, or a `Moved` with equal work); every event is idempotent in its state (evidence is not added twice, a thread ended is ended, `spent.cost` is the total so far and not an addition).
- Work that has `ended` ignores every event but `opened again`, has `next: null` and `waits: null`, and all its threads `ended`; ending is `end threads`, `end box` and a `say` to `from` naming the work and why, once each.
- Done when: `reported` done on the first step, and a person's `moved` past it, are `Refused` while `doneWhen` is `null`; `said done when` with no checks is `Refused`; with checks it is kept and said to `from` with every check's `says`; the first step is not left (by evidence) until it is said.
- A step is left when each of its `leaves` has evidence that is met and not from a doer. Then the next step is entered (an agent: `start`; a person: `ask` of need `do`, answered ends the step; the manager: it waits, `next` is `outside`), or from the last step it ends `done` if `isDone`, and else stays open.
- A doer is an agent with a thread of role `does`, by thread id or agent id. `started` makes a thread `checks` when the agent is the step's `checkedBy` agent, or the agent of a checking step (below), else `does`. `reported` by a `does` thread keeps its evidence, ends the thread, and starts a fresh `checks` thread (role `checks`, `fresh`) for an agent `checkedBy`. `checked` from any other thread, or a thread that is not there, is `Refused`.
- `isDone` needs `doneWhen`, and for each of its checks the last evidence from someone who is not a doer (a person or code counts) to be met.
- `failed`, and a `checked` with evidence that is not met, follow the step's `otherwise`: `back` sends it to that step (`sentBack[step]` plus one, the doer is told what was wrong by `start` or `tell thread`) up to `times` times, and after that asks `then.ask` (or drops with `then.drop`); `ask` asks that person; `drop` ends `dropped` with that reason. `passed` and `failed` record evidence by code, or by `{ person }` when `by` is `person` and the event has `person`.
- `unanswered`: the first time at a step the same agent is started again; the second time `answers` is asked. `stuck` asks `answers` with the agent's words. A ask's date is after the event.
- `answered` for the ask it waits for ends the wait (`chased` false); on a person's step the step is done and it goes on; elsewhere the working threads are told, or the step's agent started, with the answer. For another ask it is `Refused`. `asked` waits for that person by that date.
- `heard` on a thread it follows tells every working thread (once each); with none working and waiting for `outside` it starts the step's agent and is no longer waiting; on a thread it does not follow it is `Refused`.
- `due` before `next.by` does nothing; after it says to `from` what it waits for (the words of `next.what`) once per wait, sets `chased`, and moves `next.by` to `at` plus `look`. A new `asked` or an `answered` starts a new wait.
- `spent` with `over: null` records the total `cost`; with `over` set it asks `answers` (the words name work, manager or workspace), waits, and does not end threads, box or evidence.
- `moved` to a step of the kind starts that step (ending working threads); to another id it is `Refused`. `narrowed` to a part of the mandate is kept and tells the threads that are working; one that is wider than it is, in any action or limit, is `Refused`. `stopped` ends `stopped` and sets the mandate's state to `stopped`; `opened again` (of ended work) makes the mandate `holds` again and starts the step it was at. `handed over` ends `handed over` and says to whom and under which work id. `finished outside` ends `done` or `no` with its `says` as the reason.
- `says`: ended is _Done_, _No_, _Dropped_, _Stopped_, _Handed over_; a person it waits for (the ask, else `next`) _Waits for <name>_, `outside` _Waits for the customer_, `ci` _Waits for CI_, a manager _Waits for <name>_, `itself` _Working_; a name not in `names` is said by its id.
- Beyond `move`: on entering a step `by: { manager: true }` the manager's own doer thread (the agent of the kind's last agent step before it) is told the step's `does` as its next brief (`tell thread`, or `start` when it ended), once; the step is left when its `leaves` have evidence. A thread started or told for an agent step is given that step's `does` word for word as its `todo`.
- A checking step is a step `by` an agent whose `leaves` are not empty and all `by: "agent"`, and that no other agent is named to check (`checkedBy`: then its own agent does, and the named one looks, as in `docs/templates/customers`) (`check` in `docs/templates/repo`): entering it is a `start` with role `checks` and `fresh`, `started` makes its thread `checks` (fresh, it has not seen the doing), and its `checked` is evidence from someone who is not a doer. The checker's evidence names the done-when ids of the work and the ids of the step's `leaves`; a leave is met by the evidence that names it, and `checked` with all of it met leaves the step. A step checked by `{ code: true }` is left when its commands pass: what we ran in the box is `passed` or `failed` by `ci`.
- Sent back to a step, nothing shown before counts for its leaves, the leaves of the steps after it, or a done when that agents show: evidence that says `met: false`, by code, is added for each that was met. What was there stays, as history.
- A `command` check that fails tells the doer `<check id> failed: <run> exited <code>`, then what it printed. The work's activity says a check of CI as `Check <name> passed` or `Check <name> failed`. When the pull request is merged the issue is told once, `Merged #<pull>.`, and is closed by it.
