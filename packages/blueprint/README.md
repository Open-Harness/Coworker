# @coworker/blueprint

Co-worker's model, declared in Orthograph's `Blueprint.*`: things, values, errors, events, machines, operations, roles and stores. It is
imported but does not constrain the app yet: nothing is generated from it, and the SDK reads it only as types.

- **Owns:** what Co-worker _is_. The screens are the source; [docs/blueprint-light/inventory.md](../../docs/blueprint-light/inventory.md)
  is the evidence for every declaration.
- **Invariant:** only this package and `blueprint-delta` import `effect` or `orthograph` (`scripts/check-packages.ts`).
- **Test:** `pnpm typecheck` compiles it against the vendored language in `packages/orthograph`.

Laid out as Orthograph's `examples/work`: `things/<thing>/{thing,machine,events,errors,roles,verbs/*}.ts`, and `values/`.

screens imply that no doing registers yet, and **read** a read. A verb with neither is a write path that is not a doing
(`POST /chats/:chat`, the `/set-up/:walk` events, `POST /templates/:t/try`, `POST /api/join/request`, `POST /api/workspaces`).

**Where state lives.** No `lives` is a D1 row. `lives: "actor"` is one Durable Object per instance: a workspace, a piece of work,
a root chat with its forks. `stores/workspace.ts` and `stores/work.ts` are rows inside another thing's object (delta D3), and
`stores/vault.ts` is the vault. A manager is configuration in the workspace's object, never an actor.

**Machines are lifecycles only.** A work's step is its playbook's data, written by `work.move` with no `move:` (delta D1). Moves no
person presses (a thread's wait, wake and report; a note proposed; an ask asked) are the loop's (delta D5).

**Roles.** `member`, `builder` and `owner` in `things/workspace/roles.ts`, decided as `<role>.of("workspace")`, so every payload
names its workspace. `self` in `things/person/roles.ts`. None of them tells a person from the co-worker acting for them (delta D4).

**Exit codes**, by thing: 20s workspace, 25 to 26 person, 27 invite, 28 join, 30 to 32 co-worker, 34 note, 35 to 36 chat, 40 to 44
manager, 45 to 47 playbook, 48 comment, 50 to 51 template, 52 to 53 walk, 55 to 56 trigger, 57 item, 59 trial, 60 to 65 work, 70
thread, 75 to 78 ask, 80 call, 83 to 85 account, 86 to 87 conversation, 88 to 89 MCP, 90 to 91 environment, 95 key, 97 to 98 billing.
